CAT Z Studio Beyond Time

What's CAT Z Studio Beyond Time?

CAT Z Studio: Beyond Time is a music-creation game where you mix cats, sounds, beats, vocals, and effects to create your own songs. It expands on the original CAT Z Studio with new characters, sounds, a synchronization system, hidden easter eggs, and a more mysterious atmosphere.

How to play?

Drag and drop the sound icons onto the cats to make them perform. Click a cat to remove its sound, or hold a cat for one second to activate Solo mode. Use the Equalizer to adjust a selected cat's volume, pitch, pan, and other sound settings. The X button removes all sounds, while the ? button opens the tutorial. On mobile, use touch and drag instead of a mouse.
