CAT Z Studio - a music creating game and tool

What's CAT Z Studio?

CAT Z Studio is a music-creation game and tool where you can build your own tracks by combining different sounds represented by CAT Z characters. The game features 10 different music styles, including Trap, Ninjaz, DNB, House, Dubstep, Lo-Fi, Techno, Experimental, Dark DNB, and Chaos. You can experiment with different sound combinations, adjust individual sounds, and create your own unique mixes. CAT Z Studio is designed around creativity and experimentation, giving you the freedom to make music however you want.

How to CAT Z?

To play CAT Z Studio, first choose a music style and select the sound tiles you want to use. Drag a sound tile onto a CAT Z character to add that sound to your mix. Add more cats to combine different beats, melodies, and effects and create your track. Click a cat to remove its sound, or hold a cat for about one second to activate Solo Mode and listen to that sound on its own. You can also use the equaliser to adjust individual sounds, including their volume, pitch, and pan. When you're happy with your creation, use the Save button to save your mix. If you want to start over, use the X button to clear the current mix, and the Tutorial button can help explain the basic controls.
